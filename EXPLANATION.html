Client Job Analysis

What changed, what every number means, and how to read a row.

One example client is used throughout, so all the numbers connect.

Why we built this

Operators were adding fewer job cards to save time, and no screen anywhere showed it.

The reason nobody caught it is that we had a ceiling but no floor. The system blocked job number 31 for a client, but never once asked why only 3 were added.

It was actually worse than that. Both daily Discord reminders measured the wrong thing.

ReminderWhat it didThe side effect
8 PM
"please apply"		 Fired whenever a client had saved cards waiting. Add fewer cards, get nagged less.
1:30 PM
"zero saved"		 Fired only at exactly zero saved cards, counted over all time. One leftover card from three weeks ago switched it off forever.

So adding less made Discord quieter, not louder.

The system was rewarding exactly the behaviour we wanted to stop. That is what these changes remove.

1The "Added Today" column

This is the headline number, and the one everything else hangs off.

Added TodayReading
19/30 19 cards added today. Target is 30. Eleven short.

Left number is what was added. Right number is what was expected.

The three colours mean three different things

BadgeWhat it means
19/30 Red. Active client, below target. Someone needs to act.
30/30 Green. Active client, target met.
0/— Grey. No target applies. The client is Paused, Inactive, or still Onboarding.

Grey is the important one.

A paused client with 0 added is not a failure, so it must not look like one. It is not a success either, so it must not be green. Hover the grey badge and it tells you which of the three reasons applies.

"Today" does not mean midnight to midnight

The day runs 10 PM to 10 PM IST.

That is not arbitrary. It is exactly the window the push cap already uses, so this number always matches the counter the extension shows the operator. If the two disagreed, nobody would trust either.

a card added 10:30 PM Monday night  →  counts towards Tuesday

2"STALE 3d"

Added TodayReading
0/30STALE 3d Nothing at all has been added for 3 days.

STALE is about silence, not about being short. It only appears when a client has had a completely empty day or more.

What you seeWhat it means
3/30 Someone worked on them today, just not enough. No STALE.
0/30STALE 1d Nobody touched them today or yesterday.
0/30STALE 6d This client has been forgotten for most of a week.

3"7d Avg"

Average cards added per day over the last 7 completed days. Today is deliberately left out: a day that is only two hours old would drag the average down and make every client look bad every morning.

Use it to answer one question: is today normal for this client?

Added 3  ·  7d avg 28

Something broke today. Yesterday was fine.

Added 3  ·  7d avg 4

This client is neglected every day.

Same shortfall. Completely different conversation.

There is also a Yday column showing yesterday's count on its own. It is currently switched off to save screen width. Search Yday — HIDDEN in ClientJobAnalysis.jsx to bring it back.

Click a pillFilters the table to that problem.
Hide for this session Hides it until you next open the browser. It comes back tomorrow on purpose, so dismissing it can never switch it off for good.

There is also a small ⚠ next to the client name in the table itself, so a flagged client stays obvious once you scroll past the banner. Hover it to read why.

The two alerts

AlertFires whenTurns critical
No jobs added Nothing added for a full day or more. At 2 days. By then it survived a whole cycle of reminders and nobody acted.
Saved but not applied Cards are waiting in the saved column and nothing was applied today. Once the last application was 2 or more days ago.

The second one is new information.

The old 8 PM reminder said "please apply" without ever checking whether anyone did. A backlog could sit untouched for a week while Discord repeated the identical message every evening.

It stays quiet for clients we owe nothing.

Paused, Inactive and Onboarding clients never raise an alert. They legitimately get nothing added, and flagging them would bury the real signal and train everyone to ignore the panel.

"Added Today says 19 but Saved only says 17. Where did 2 go?"

They count different things.

Added Today (19) Cards created today, whatever happened to them afterwards.
Saved (17) Cards sitting right now in the saved column.

The answer is in the same row. Removed by AI: 2, and that is also a today number. The AI pulled 2 of today's 19 straight back out, either an expired posting or an exclusion-list match.

19 added today  −  2 removed by AI today  =  17 waiting in saved

Nothing is wrong. The numbers agree.

"Total Apps says 135 but the columns add up to more."

Total Apps is Saved + Applied + Interview + Offer + Rejected.

17  +  117  +  1  +  0  +  0  =  135

The 53 Removed are deliberately left out, so this client has actually had 188 cards created in their lifetime.

And the warning triangle?

17 cards are waiting and nothing went out today. That is the "saved but not applied" alert. The 117 applied is a lifetime figure spread over 13 days, not today's work.

6The 9:30 PM Discord report

A new daily message listing every active client who is short, worst first, naming who worked on them today.

Add shortfall — 2026-08-20 (window closes 22:00 IST)
18 of 42 active clients are under target. 312 job cards added today.

• Rahul Sharma — 0/30 (0%) · today by: nobody · no adds for 2d
• Aysha Nijhawan — 19/30 (63%) · today by: Priya
• Neha Verma — 22/30 (73%) · today by: Arjun, Kabir

Why 9:30 PM and not later?

The day closes at 10 PM. Sending at 9:30 leaves half an hour to fix things. A report that lands after the day is already lost just gets muted.

To switch it on: set DISCORD_ADD_SHORTFALL to a Discord webhook URL.

Use a separate channel from the other two reminders. This one is a scoreboard a team lead reads top to bottom, not a per-client nag. Without that variable the report simply does not run; everything else works regardless.

7The two old reminders, fixed

1:30 PM "zero saved"

Used to fire only at exactly zero saved cards, counted over all time. It now also fires when nothing was added today, however big the backlog is.

8 PM "please apply"

Now states the day's adds in the same breath:

Hi Priya, there are 17 job card(s) in saved column for Aysha's dashboard, please apply.
Added today: 19/30 — 11 short of target.

Clearing the backlog no longer reads as a finished day.

"Applied Today" now shows something useful

This column used to read "Applied on Date" and show 0 for every client when no date was picked, which told you nothing. It now shows the applications that actually went out today, and turns amber when it is 0 while cards are waiting.

Filters and sorting

Sort (Added Today header) Cycles biggest shortfall first, smallest first, off.
Dropdown (Added Today header) Under target  ·  No adds for 1d+  ·  Target met
Counters (top of page) 14 Under target   6 No adds 1d+   312 Added today — the first two are clickable.

Fixed quietly along the way

  • Two scheduled jobs claimed an IST time in their comments but passed no timezone, so they silently depended on the server running in UTC. Now stated explicitly.
  • The page caches its data for speed, but the cache had no idea when we changed the code, so newly added columns stayed blank until midnight. It now carries a version stamp and refreshes the moment new code goes live.
  • A database index was added so the new counts do not slow the page down.

Checking the raw data yourself

To see cards added per day straight from the database:

cd clients-tracking/applications_monitor_backend
export $(grep -m1 ^MONGODB_URI .env)
node scripts/check-add-rate.mjs 14

A quiet day is not always someone slacking.

Three things can silently block adds: a client hitting their lifetime plan limit, a database hiccup refusing pushes, or an expired extension session key. The script reminds you of all three. Rule them out before drawing conclusions about effort.
